UNDERTOW - bassline generator by Token Wise (VST3 + AU instrument, Apple Silicon + Intel)

INSTALL
  1. Right-click "Install Undertow.command" > Open > Open
  2. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins. Ableton: it shows up under Plug-ins.
  If macOS refuses, open Terminal and paste:
     cd ~/Downloads/Undertow && xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine . && ./"Install Undertow.command"

USE
  Load Undertow as an instrument on a new track and press play in your DAW
  (or hit the play button inside the plugin to preview without the DAW playing).

  1. Pick a STYLE: Rolling, Bouncy, Offbeat, 2-Step, Minimal, Driving, Broken, Acid.
  2. Set KEY, SCALE, OCT and BARS at the top.
  3. Hit GENERATE until you like it. MUTATE nudges a few notes, UNDO goes back.
     LOCK RHYTHM keeps the groove and rolls new notes, LOCK PITCH does the opposite.
  4. SHAPE: Density (how many notes), Movement (how far it leaves the root),
     Variation (how different each bar is), Length (short and plucky vs long).
  5. FEEL: Swing and Glide. SOUND: Sub, Rubber, Reese, Organ, Acid, Growl + Tone, Drive.
     KICK plays a preview kick and ducks the bass so you hear the groove. Turn it off in your track.

  GET THE MIDI
    Drag the DRAG MIDI tile straight onto a track in your DAW, or hit EXPORT to save a .mid.
    Undertow also sends its MIDI out, so you can route it into Serpent or any synth.
